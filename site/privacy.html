Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Sunset Mountain Fish and Game Club Inc. Dues Invoicer ("the App") is a small internal tool used by officers of Sunset Mountain Fish and Game Club Inc. ("the Club") to create and send annual membership dues invoices in the Club's own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the public.

Where the App runs

The App runs on a computer controlled by a Club officer. The Club does not operate a web server, database, or cloud service for the App, and the App has no user accounts or public sign-up.

What the App accesses

With the officer's authorization, the App reads from the Club's QuickBooks Online company: customer (member) names, customer IDs, and email addresses; product and service names and prices; existing invoice references (to avoid creating duplicates); and company payment settings. It creates invoices and asks QuickBooks to email them to members.

What the App stores

On the officer's computer only, the App stores: (1) an authorization token file that lets it reconnect to QuickBooks, (2) CSV log files listing which members were invoiced, the amounts, and whether each invoice was emailed, and (3) a text log of each run, including any error messages and Intuit support trace IDs, kept for troubleshooting. Nothing else is stored.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone. The App sends data only to Intuit/QuickBooks, the service the Club already uses for its books. The App has no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Local files remain until the officer deletes them. The Club may disconnect the App from QuickBooks at any time (see the Disconnect page), which ends the App's access. Deleting the token file and log files removes everything the App stored.

Security

The App connects to QuickBooks over encrypted (HTTPS) connections using Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization. The token file is saved with permissions restricted to the computer's user account. Officers are expected to keep the token file, the app's Client Secret, and exported member lists private.
