Disconnect from QuickBooks

You can end the Dues Invoicer's access to the Club's QuickBooks company at any time:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an admin.
  2. Open the Apps menu and choose My apps.
  3. Find the Dues Invoicer and select Disconnect.

The app can no longer reach the company's data once disconnected. To also remove what it stored, delete the saved token file (qbo_tokens.json) and any dues_preview_*.csv and dues_run_*.csv log files on the officer's computer.
