Disconnect from QuickBooks

You can end the Dues Invoicer's access to the Club's QuickBooks company at any time, in either of two ways.

From the app

On the officer's computer, run the app with the --disconnect option. It revokes its access with Intuit and deletes its saved token file.

From QuickBooks

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an admin.
  2. Open the Apps area and find the Dues Invoicer among the connected apps.
  3. Select Disconnect.

The app can no longer reach the company's data once disconnected. To also remove anything it stored, delete the saved token file (qbo_tokens.json) and any dues_preview_*.csv, dues_run_*.csv, and dues_log_*.txt log files on the officer's computer.
