Connect or reconnect to QuickBooks

Only authorized officers of Sunset Mountain Fish and Game Club Inc. should connect the app.

  1. On the officer's computer, run the Dues Invoicer. When it needs access, it prints a link to Intuit's authorization page.
  2. Open the link and sign in to QuickBooks Online with the Club's account.
  3. Choose the Club's company and select Connect.
  4. Copy the full address from the page you land on and paste it into the Dues Invoicer when asked.

The app then saves an authorization token on that computer. If the connection expires or is revoked, repeat these steps to reconnect.
